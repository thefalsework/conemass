# OpenRefactory's PyPI scans vs. conemass, population scale

Generated by `analyze-pypi.py` from `repo-packages.csv` and `pypi-edges.csv` (`fetch-pypi.py`, deps.dev) and the public results sheet.

Population graph: 4233 PyPI packages, 16454 resolved dependency edges (union of deps.dev's resolved graph for each scanned package at its audited or audit-date version). 2267 scanned repositories map to a resolvable PyPI package (413 of those packages have no edge at all and sit at the bottom of every ordering); a repository with several packages is represented by its best-ranked one.
